## 1. Context

The user optimized **supply, combat, and movement** in the last several commits
(`9c0a52c` "Optimize supply massively", `bf9e373` "Rewrite Spatialty into
NearbyEnemies", `f1331c9` "tickcontext rebuild on demand", `12dcf02`
"unit_types_index to movement", `1518062` "reinforce into supply"). Those
commits established the house patterns: **reuse pre-grouped `TickContext`
data, use scratch buffers, `Arc::clone` instead of `world_data_clone`, skip
work that isn't needed, shrink value types.** This plan applies the same
treatment to the **influence** system, and — where tightly coupled —
**encirclement**.

Baseline (`reports/report_72.html`, 1500 ticks, seed 42, 4 players, ~200×200):

| System | Total | Calls | Avg | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| influence | 27.4 ms | 566 | 0.048 ms | every 3 ticks |
| encirclement | 8.8 ms | 56 | 0.156 ms | every 30 ticks |

Prior round (already done): flat arrays, precomputed `circle_spans`, reverse
flood-fill, single dominant-player grid. **This round's problem:** per-call heap
churn (~0.8 MB/call at 200×200, P=4), redundant full recompute on unchanged
ticks, and an oversized `u32` grid type. Research found the two grid zero-inits
(~200 KB/player memset) dominate the measured 48 µs/call — so the biggest wins
are *not computing at all* and *halving the buffer*, not micro-optimizing the
span math.

## 2. Approach

**Included (decisions + rationale):**

1. **`u32` → `u16` influence grid — INCLUDE.** Halves the largest per-player
   buffer (160 KB → 80 KB at 200×200): halves zero-init stores, store bandwidth,
   and encirclement's read of each grid. The **only** in-game consumer is the
   strict-`>` dominant-grid pass (encirclement.rs). Review verified the *only*
   flat `borders.grids` readers in the whole codebase are `game.rs:1148`
   (`border_grid_2d`) and `encirclement.rs:22`, with `influence.rs:235` the sole
   writer. Every external consumer (`server/protocol.rs:9,19`,
   `server/delta.rs:81-83,110`, `game_server.rs`, `llm/agent.rs:45`,
   `replay.rs:178`) is 2D `Vec<Vec<u32>>` derived from `border_grid_2d`/
   `supervisor_border_grid`, so a **single widening at `border_grid_2d`** keeps
   the wire/LLM/replay protocol byte-identical. No server/llm/replay edits
   needed. `u8` rejected (saturates at 256; densest documented fixture is 500
   stacked units → cell ≈ 500 + 5·cities, 3 orders of magnitude under `u16::MAX`
   = 65535).

2. **Fold vision into the circle span loop — INCLUDE, scoped.** Flat units
   (square r3 ⊆ circle r5) and cities (square r4 ⊆ circle r7) set
   `vision = true` inside the existing span loop, deleting their separate square
   passes (~38% fewer unit-loop cells, ~35% fewer city cells). Review verified
   the containment is **bit-for-bit** where square ⊆ circle and `circle_spans`
   covers exactly `dx²+dy² ≤ r²`. **Hill units keep the separate square pass**
   (r6 ⊄ r5 — folding would shrink hill vision, a behavioral change): the fold
   is gated on `v_radius <= i_radius` with an `else` branch preserving today's
   two passes exactly for hills. Removes dead `_max_radius`.

3. **Skip-when-clean via persistent `influence_dirty` — INCLUDE, with 5 dirty
   sources and the recruit/death staleness fixes.** The per-tick `ctx_dirty`
   (reset at game.rs:493) cannot express "changed since last influence run." A
   persistent `Game` flag, cleared only when influence actually runs, skips
   compute + all per-player allocs on clean ticks. Sources (death, recruit,
   assign_city_owner) **also fix real pre-existing staleness bugs**: today a
   recruit, a death, or an `assign_city_owner` leaves `ctx.units_by_player` /
   `ctx.cities` stale until an unrelated unit moves on a consumer tick, so grids
   silently omit the new unit, keep painting the dead one, or freeze on wrong
   ownership. Encirclement reuses a derived `encirclement_dirty` (Step 8).

4. **`PlayerInput`/`PlayerOutput` elimination + `InfluenceScratch` — INCLUDE.**
   `run()` iterates `ctx.units_by_player` / `ctx.cities` directly (entities carry
   `pos` via `UnitHandle`), killing the per-call `unit_positions`/`city_positions`
   `collect()`s, `player_id.to_string()`, and the **Phase-3 double clone**
   (influence.rs:224, verified exact). Trade-off accepted: lose the dead-Rayon
   pure-function surface (Rayon is already rejected per influence.rs:213-216);
   the core stays a pure-ish `compute_player` taking references so it remains
   unit-testable.

5. **Hygiene — INCLUDE.** Hoist the row-level `y < h` test above the dx loop
   (constant per row); delete `_max_radius` and `let x_base = ux_i`; keep terrain
   as `[x][y]` and grids flat `[y*width+x]`.

6. **Benchmark repair — INCLUDE.** Move `TickContext::build_full` out of the
   timed closures (it currently measures rebuild too) into fixture setup, and
   add a quiet (skip-path) microbench since the full-game battle benchmark moves
   every unit every tick and would show ~0 from the skip.

**Excluded:** re-introducing Rayon; grid downsampling/interpolation;
vision-radius rebalancing (hills keep r6); `u8` grids; changing
`WorldBundle` grid storage shape; wire-protocol changes; per-cell dirty
tracking to skip the `fill()` memsets.
